FAQ

A few

Is online learning really effective for young children?+

Yes. ZUZO is built specifically around online teaching, with one-to-one lessons, practice submissions, feedback, assessments and active parent communication.

How long is each lesson?+

Lessons are available in 35-minute and 45-minute formats. One lesson a week is the usual starting point; twice weekly is recommended for more serious study.

Can beginners start?+

Absolutely. We teach beginners through advanced students, from age six through adults.

Do you prepare students for exams?+

Yes. We support structured preparation for Trinity, ABRSM and Rockschool pathways.

Where can I take lessons from?+

Anywhere with a reliable internet connection. ZUZO is currently online and serves students internationally.
