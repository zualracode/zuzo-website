QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before we
make music.

Can children really learn music effectively online?+

Yes. ZUZO was specifically designed around the online format, with one-to-one teaching, practice submissions, feedback, assessments and parent communication.

Does my child need prior experience?+

No. Beginners are welcome. We meet students at their current level and build from there.

How long are lessons?+

Lessons are available in 35-minute and 45-minute formats. One lesson per week is the usual starting point; twice weekly is recommended for students pursuing music more seriously.

Do you prepare students for exams?+

Yes. Students can work toward recognised pathways including Trinity, ABRSM and Rockschool where appropriate.

Where are lessons available?+

ZUZO teaches online, so students can learn from anywhere. Our international focus includes the UK, US and Middle East.
