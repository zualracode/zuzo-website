What ages do you teach?+

Students from approximately 6 years old through adults and retirees.

Are lessons one-to-one?+

Yes. Every lesson is individual and tailored to the student's level and goals.

How long are lessons?+

Lessons are available in 35-minute and 45-minute formats.

Can students prepare for exams?+

Yes. We support Trinity, ABRSM and Rockschool pathways.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes. The first trial lesson is free and lasts 30 minutes.
